NotePress – Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026. NotePress is made by NTQ37.

Your music stays on your computer

The AI that finds the notes runs on your own computer, and everything it needs comes with the app. Your recordings, notes and sheet music are stored only in this app on your device. They are never uploaded to NTQ37 or anyone else.

Microphone

The microphone is used only while you record, after you allow it. The recording is saved on your computer only.

Purchases

NotePress Pro is bought through the Microsoft Store. NTQ37 does not receive your payment details; the app only asks the Store whether Pro is owned.

No accounts, no tracking

There is no account, no advertising and no analytics.
