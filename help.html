How to use NotePress

1. Add music

Click ＋ Add music or drop a recording on the window (MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC, OGG or a video), or 🎙 Record yourself playing or singing. NotePress works best with one instrument: for a full band song, first separate the piano, guitar or voice with StemPress.

2. Write down the notes

Choose the instrument (this sets the range), tick One note at a time for singing or a melody, and click 🎵 Write down the notes. Notes found finds more (or fewer) quiet notes; Shortest note removes tiny notes.

3. Check and fix

Press ▶ to hear the original and the notes together – the Original ⟷ Notes slider mixes them. In the piano roll, click a note to choose it, press Delete to remove it or ↑ ↓ to move it.

4. Sheet music and files

🎼 Sheet music shows the score with the tempo, time signature and key found automatically – change them if needed. Save a MIDI file for any music program, or (Pro) MusicXML for MuseScore, Sibelius, Finale and Dorico, and print or save the sheet music as PDF.
